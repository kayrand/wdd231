The Recipe Book

Site Plan · Kaylee Godfrey · WDD 231

Site Name

The Recipe Book

I went with "The Recipe Book" because that's really what the site is. It's my own collection of recipes in one place. The name feels cozy and homey, like a recipe book you'd actually keep in the kitchen, and it makes it obvious right away what the site is about.

Site Purpose

The Recipe Book is a collection of comfort food and easy, homestyle recipes. Here's what people can do on the site:

  • Look through a bunch of recipes laid out as cards.
  • Filter recipes by category and click one to see the full ingredients and directions.
  • Add their own recipes with a form, and those get saved so they're still there next time they visit.

I want it to be a warm, easy place where people can find recipes they'll actually want to make and start building their own collection.

Scenarios

Here are a couple of questions someone visiting the site might have:

Scenario 1

"I need to make dinner tonight but I don't have much time. What can I make that's quick and easy?"

Scenario 2

"I found a recipe I love. How can I save my own recipes so I can come back to them later?"

Color Scheme

The site uses three main colors:

Teal
#3D5A5B
Rust
#C4703E
Tan
#F5EBDD

Teal (#3D5A5B) is for the header, footer, and the main headings.
Rust (#C4703E) is for the buttons, links, and little accent details.
Tan (#F5EBDD) is the page background, which gives it a warm, paper-like feel.

Typography

I'm using two fonts:

Patrick Hand for the site name, headings, and recipe titles

Nunito Sans for the body text, recipe details, and buttons, so everything stays clear and easy to read.
