Discover Birmingham

Local History

Founded in 1871, Birmingham grew rapidly as an industrial center known for iron and steel production, earning it the nickname "The Magic City." The famous Vulcan statue, the largest cast-iron statue in the world, still watches over the city today.

Demographics

Birmingham is the most populous city in Alabama, with a metro area home to over one million people. It is a growing hub for medical research, banking, and education.

Current Events

From festivals at Railroad Park to events at the Birmingham Museum of Art, there is always something happening. Visit local venues year-round for music, food, and community celebrations.
